1# SubAquea

info_SubAquea:

The SubAquea ceramic collection draws inspiration from "Toledo" metro station of Napoli "Linea 1" to develop forms that embody the aesthetics and design of the iconic station.

info_SubAquea:

Initially, the forms took shape based on elements that characterise the station, such as the skylights, the waves along the escalators, and the corridor ceiling.

Following a process of simplification and reinterpretation of these forms, we wanted the collection to faithfully represent the central element that unites all components of the station: the sea.
