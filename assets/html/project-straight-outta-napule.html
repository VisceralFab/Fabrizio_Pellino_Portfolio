3# Straight Outta Napule

info_StraightOuttaNapule:

Straight Outta Napule is a short-film that won the Afragola Film Festival. This project showcases storytelling through cinematography, capturing the essence and culture of Naples.

info_Straight_Outta_Napule:

Filmed together with Vincenzo Zucco, the short film unfolds across three distinct neighbourhoods identified by their postal codes: Centro Storico, Fuorigrotta and Bagnoli. The first two represent Vincenzo's and my respective places of belonging, while the third, Bagnoli, is a territory we both share and have chosen to film.

The production process involved extensive location scouting across the three neighbourhoods to capture the unique character and atmosphere of each area. Every frame was carefully composed to reflect the cultural richness and historical significance of these distinct regions of Naples.
