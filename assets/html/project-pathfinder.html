6# Pathfinder

info_Pathfinder:

Pathfinder is a UI-UX Design project developed during the Apple Foundation Program at the Apple Developer Academy in Naples. This project focuses on creating intuitive and engaging user experiences through thoughtful interface design.

info_Pathfinder:

Pathfinder and the Apple Foundation Program experience represented a significant opportunity for me as a UI/UX designer, given Apple's importance in the design world. The Apple Developer Academy allowed people like me to experiment with developing applications and interfaces using professional tools, simulating the experience of working for a major client.
